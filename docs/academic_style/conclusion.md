# Conclusion

## Moves
1. The answer to the study aim, in one or two sentences, at the strength the design allows.
2. The practical implication or the specific next step.
Length: 2-4 sentences (about 50-100 words). Some journals fold it into the Discussion.

## Rules
- No new data, no numbers that need a confidence interval, no citations.
- Mirrors the aim sentence of the Introduction and the conclusion of the Abstract.
- No promotional adjectives ("promising", "excellent", "safe and effective") unless the data
  measured exactly that.

## Phrasebank
- "In patients with ..., X was associated with ... compared with Y."
- "These findings support ... ; a randomized trial with ... is needed to ..."

## Model paragraph
In patients with lumbar spinal stenosis, endoscopic decompression was associated with fewer
reoperations for instability at 5 years than microscopic decompression. A randomized trial with
reoperation as the primary outcome would establish whether this association is causal.
