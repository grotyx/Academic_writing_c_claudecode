# Title

## Rules
- State the population, intervention or exposure, comparator and outcome, and the design when
  the journal asks for it ("...: a randomized controlled trial").
- 10-15 words; no abbreviations except universally known ones; no question titles and no
  declarative result titles unless the journal prefers them.
- Running title: within the journal limit, usually 40-50 characters.

## Phrasebank
- "X versus Y for Z: a retrospective cohort study"
- "Reoperation after endoscopic or microscopic decompression for lumbar spinal stenosis"
- "Association between X and Y in patients with Z"

## Avoid -> prefer
- "A novel and effective technique for ..." -> name the technique and the comparison.
- "Is X better than Y?" -> "X versus Y for ...".
